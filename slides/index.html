How AI Thinks · Step 1

The Secret: Numbers

cat

When you see this word,
what happens in your brain?

Your brain sees...

Soft fur Purring Cuddly Whiskers Pointy ears Playful Warm & sleepy Sneezy if allergic!

Your brain floods with memories, images, and feelings
— all at once, instantly.

But AI can't do any of that.

It can't picture a cat. It can't feel fur. It can't hear purring.

All it sees is numbers:

"cat" → 12,288 numbers
0.382-0.7410.156-0.893 0.524-0.0670.918-0.445 0.271-0.6320.089-0.553 0.716-0.1820.447...
Wow Fact
12,288
numbers per word

Imagine covering an entire basketball court with tennis balls, packed side by side. That's about 12,000 balls — almost exactly how many numbers AI uses for a single word like "cat".

How AI Thinks · Step 2

Words in Space

Those 12,288 numbers act like coordinates,
placing each word in a giant space.

Similar words end up close together.

🐾 Animals 👑 Royalty 🍚 Food 💻 Tech cat dog bird fish king queen prince pizza burger sushi computer robot phone

Similar ideas cluster together in vector space!

The Payoff

Every word = a mix of ALL dimensions at once:

cat = [animal: high, fur: high, wings: zero, rideable: zero, ...]

× 12,288 dimensions

Wow Fact
~100,000 tokens
in AI's vocabulary — roughly 3× more than the average adult's 20,000–35,000 words. Every single token gets its own 12,288 coordinates.

How AI Thinks · Step 3

Attention!

bat

What does this word mean?

🥏

Baseball bat

🦇

Flying animal

Trick question — both are right!

AI's solution: pay attention to the surrounding words.

Context is everything.

Sentence 1:

I swung the bat and hit the ball!

🥏 Baseball bat!
"Swung," "hit," "ball" — all baseball words.

Sentence 2:

The bat flew out of the cave at dusk.

🦇 Flying animal!
"Flew," "cave," "dusk" — nature words.

Same word. Different spotlight.

The meaning changes depending on which words
are shining their spotlight on it.

Wow Fact
96 attention heads
Claude runs 96 different spotlights simultaneously — each looking for different types of relationships. Like 96 readers, each hunting for something different in the same sentence.

How AI Thinks · Step 4

The Thinking Layer

Attention figured out which words relate.
Now the MLP does the heavy thinking.

Input
12,288

49,152

Output
12,288

Attention = Reading the room
MLP = Flipping through your entire memory

Like breathing in deeply before answering a hard question.
"Expand then compress" gives the model room to think.

How AI Thinks · Step 5

Rinse & Repeat

One round of Attention + MLP isn't enough.

The model does it 96 TIMES.

What each layer range learns:

Layers 1–16: Letters & Spelling
Layers 17–32: Words & Grammar
Layers 33–48: Facts & Knowledge
Layers 49–64: Context & Tone
Layers 65–80: Logic & Reasoning
Layers 81–96: Deep Understanding

From mechanical (letters) to abstract (metaphor, nuance) —
each layer builds on the last.

How AI Thinks · Step 6

Predict!

All those 96 layers —
just to pick one word.

Then it does it all over again for the next word,
and the next, and the next...

The Temperature Dial

Controls how adventurous or safe the AI is:

❄️
Frozen
Always picks the top word. Perfectly predictable.
⚖️
Balanced
Mostly sensible, occasionally creative.
🔥
Warm
Getting adventurous! Surprises start appearing.
🌊
Wild
Full chaos — literally anything could come next!

Example

"The sleepy cat sat on a warm ___"

mat (72%)
cushion (18%)
rug (6%)
volcano (0.1%)

Low temperature ❄️ → always "mat"
High temperature 🔥 → maybe "volcano" 😄

This is why AI gives a different answer every time!

Wow Fact
5,760+
layer passes for a typical 60-word paragraph

Running all 96 layers, 60 times over.
A 9-word sentence = 864 layer passes.
All just to say "good morning."
